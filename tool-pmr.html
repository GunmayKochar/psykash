Progressive Muscle Relaxation

A systematic exercise to release deep physical tension and signal to your brain that it is time to sleep.

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does the whole routine take?

A full body scan usually takes about 10 to 15 minutes, but you can do a shortened version focusing only on your hands, shoulders, and face in 2 minutes.

Is it normal to fall asleep before finishing?

Yes! That is often the goal. If you fall asleep halfway through, the technique has done its job perfectly.

Can I do this sitting at my desk?

Yes. While lying down is ideal for sleep, you can discreetly tense and release your legs, hands, and shoulders while sitting in a chair at work.

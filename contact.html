Questions

FAQ

How do I book an appointment?

You can book an appointment by reaching out directly via the Email contact option above. We will reply with available time slots and intake forms.

How long does it take to receive a reply?

We aim to respond to all enquiries within 24 to 48 hours during regular business days.

Are therapy sessions online?

Yes, all therapy sessions are currently conducted online via secure video conferencing, allowing you to participate from the comfort of your own home.

What should I include in my first email?

Please include a brief summary of what you are looking for help with, your time zone, and your general availability for a session.

Ready to take the first step?

If you're ready to begin therapy or have any questions, feel free to reach out via email or connect through Instagram or LinkedIn.

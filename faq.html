Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions from across all our self-help guides and quick toolkit techniques.

How many times should I repeat the cycle?

Aim for at least 4 cycles to start feeling the physiological effects. You can continue for up to 5-10 minutes if needed.

Is it dangerous to hold my breath?

A gentle 4-second hold is completely safe for most people and is actually the mechanism that triggers the calming vagal response. However, if you feel dizzy, return to normal breathing immediately.

Can I do this lying down?

Yes! Box breathing is highly effective when lying in bed trying to fall asleep, as it mimics the respiratory rate of a sleeping body.

What if I can't find 5 things to see?

You can count the same object's different properties—like five different colors on a single poster, or five different shadows on the floor. It's about the focus, not the objects themselves.

Does this cure anxiety?

No, grounding is a coping mechanism for acute distress. It lowers the immediate intensity of a panic attack so you can function and employ longer-term emotional regulation strategies.

Can I do this in a crowded room?

Absolutely. It is entirely discreet. You can look around, touch your own clothing, listen to the ambient noise, and take a sip of water without anyone knowing you are doing a grounding exercise.

What if I need my phone for work emergencies in the bedroom?

Leave the phone plugged in just outside the bedroom door with the ringer on loud for calls only. You will hear an emergency call, but you won't be able to scroll in bed.

Can I reinstall the app after the 7 days?

Yes, but you will likely find you no longer want to. If you do, reinstall it but keep it off the home screen.

What do I do with the extra time?

At first, you will feel bored and restless. This is normal withdrawal. Let yourself be bored. Read, walk, or talk to someone.

Will a vacation cure my burnout?

A vacation offers temporary relief, but if you return to the exact same boundaries and workload, the burnout will return within weeks. Recovery requires systemic changes to your routine.

How long does it take to recover?

Recovery timeline varies depending on severity, but severe burnout can take months or even a year to fully resolve. Patience and radical rest are essential.

What if I can't afford to quit my job?

Quitting is not the only option. Focus on things you can control: strictly limiting overtime, taking full lunch breaks away from your desk, and emotionally detaching your self-worth from your productivity.

Is all comparison bad?

No. Upward comparison can be motivating if it inspires you to improve. It only becomes toxic when it generates feelings of inadequacy and shame.

Why do I compare myself to people I do not even like?

Because your brain is assessing social hierarchy, not personal affection. It is a biological reflex looking for status threats, even among strangers.

How do I handle family members who compare me to others?

Set a firm boundary. You can calmly say, "I am focusing on my own path right now, and I would appreciate it if we didn't compare my progress to others."

Is it wrong to want to be perfect?

Striving for excellence is healthy. Perfectionism, however, is a defense mechanism rooted in the fear of judgment. True excellence requires making mistakes; perfectionism forbids them.

How do I stop caring what people think if I fail?

Recognise that most people are far too consumed by their own fears and insecurities to spend much time judging yours. When they do judge, it is usually a projection of their own limitations.

What if the failure really was a big deal?

Acknowledge the weight of it, take accountability where necessary, and grieve the loss. But remember that no single event, no matter how catastrophic, defines your entire worth as a human being.

How many hours of screen time is healthy?

There is no magic number. It depends entirely on what you are doing (creating vs consuming) and whether it interferes with your sleep, relationships, or work.

Should I delete all my social media apps?

Only if you want to. For most people, simply moving the apps off the home screen or setting app timers is enough friction to break the habit without totally disconnecting.

Why do I scroll when I am stressed?

Scrolling is a highly effective, low-effort numbing mechanism. It temporarily distracts you from the stressful feeling, though it does not resolve the underlying cause.

Is it normal to lose motivation halfway through a project?

Yes. This is often called the 'messy middle'. The novelty has worn off, but the reward of completion is still far away. This is when systems and discipline must take over from excitement.

How do I get motivated to exercise?

Stop relying on feeling motivated. Pack your gym bag the night before, put it by the door, and focus only on the action of putting on your shoes. Make the barrier to entry as low as possible.

Why do I self-sabotage when things are going well?

Success can sometimes feel unfamiliar or create pressure to maintain that new standard. This fear of the unknown can cause us to subconsciously retreat to our familiar comfort zone.

Is overthinking a mental illness?

No, overthinking itself is not a mental illness. It is a very common thought pattern. However, chronic overthinking can be a symptom of anxiety disorders.

How do I stop overthinking at night?

Practicing a "brain dump" by writing down all your worries before getting into bed is one of the most effective ways to signal to your brain that it is safe to rest.

Can overthinking cause physical symptoms?

Yes. The stress caused by overthinking triggers cortisol and adrenaline release, which can cause muscle tension, headaches, and fatigue.

Is it really an addiction?

While 'addiction' is a strong word, problematic smartphone use shares many behavioral pathways with traditional addictions, including withdrawal and tolerance.

Do I have to quit social media entirely?

Not necessarily. Digital wellbeing is about intentionality. The goal is to use your phone as a tool, rather than letting it use you.

Will people dislike me if I start saying no?

Some people who benefited from your lack of boundaries may push back initially. However, healthy people will respect your limits. Boundaries act as a filter for who genuinely cares about you.

Is it selfish to put my needs first?

No. You cannot pour from an empty cup. Protecting your energy ensures that when you do choose to help others, you are doing it from a place of genuine willingness, not resentment.

How do I say no to my boss?

Focus on priorities rather than refusal. Try: "I can absolutely take on this new project. To make sure it gets done well, which of my current tasks should I deprioritize to make room for it?"

Is it better to just quit completely?

For some, yes. But for others, social media is a vital tool for staying in touch. You do not have to quit; you just have to curate it ruthlessly.

Why do I care about likes from strangers?

Likes trigger dopamine, mimicking social approval. It is a biological hack that makes you care about the opinions of people you don't even know.

How do I explain to a friend that I muted them?

You rarely have to explain, as they are not notified. If asked, say: "I am taking a step back from looking at feeds right now for my mental health, but I still want to talk to you directly!"

Why do panic attacks happen out of nowhere?

They are often a delayed release of accumulated stress. Your nervous system is overflowing, and a minor trigger (even a change in temperature or heart rate) can tip the bucket.

How do I know it is a panic attack and not a heart attack?

While the symptoms overlap, panic attacks peak within 10 minutes and then subside, and the pain is usually sharp and localized. However, if you are ever unsure, always seek medical attention first.

Will I be exhausted afterwards?

Yes, a "panic hangover" is very common. Your body just ran a chemical marathon. Drink water, rest, and be gentle with yourself for the rest of the day.

What if an area of my body is injured?

Skip any muscle groups that are injured, painful, or recovering from surgery. Focus on the surrounding healthy muscles instead.

Is it normal to feel a tingling sensation?

Yes, a warm, heavy, or slightly tingling sensation is completely normal and is a sign that the muscles are deeply relaxing and blood flow is increasing.

Can I listen to music while doing this?

Yes, soft, instrumental music without a driving beat can enhance the relaxation effect.

Does this take a long time to do?

No, the entire S.T.O.P. cycle can be completed in as little as 10 to 15 seconds. It is a micro-intervention.

What if I am too overwhelmed to observe my thoughts?

If internal observation is too overwhelming, focus entirely on external observation. Notice the temperature of the room or the color of the wall in front of you.

Can children use this technique?

Yes, it is highly effective for children and is often taught in schools to help with emotional regulation and impulse control.

How to Manage Anxiety

Practical tools to navigate anxious feelings, calm your nervous system, and regain a sense of control.

Why Does This Happen?

Anxiety is your body's natural alarm system. Evolutionarily, it was designed to keep us safe from physical danger by preparing us to fight or flee.

However, in modern life, this alarm system often triggers in response to psychological stressors—like work deadlines, social situations, or financial worries. Your brain cannot tell the difference between a physical threat and an emotional one, so it floods your body with adrenaline and cortisol, leaving you feeling on edge.

Signs You Might Relate

  • I often feel a tight sensation in my chest or a racing heart.
  • I find myself constantly dreading the future.
  • I struggle to concentrate because my mind is jumping from fear to fear.
  • I feel physically exhausted even when I haven't done much.
  • I avoid certain situations because I'm afraid they will make me anxious.

Practical Self-Help Guide

Here are five evidence-informed strategies you can use to manage anxiety when it arises.

Tip 1

The 5-4-3-2-1 Grounding Technique

Anxiety pulls you into the future. Grounding pulls you back to the present by engaging your physical senses.

Try this today

Find 5 things you can see, 4 you can touch, 3 you can hear, 2 you can smell, and 1 you can taste.

Tip 2

Lengthen Your Exhale

Breathing in stimulates the sympathetic nervous system (fight or flight). Breathing out stimulates the parasympathetic nervous system (rest and digest).

Try this today

Breathe in for 4 seconds, and breathe out slowly for 6 seconds. Repeat for two minutes.

Tip 3

Label the Feeling

Research shows that simply putting a name to an emotion reduces the activity in the amygdala (the brain's fear centre).

Try this today

Say out loud to yourself: "I am noticing that I feel anxious right now."

Tip 4

Limit Caffeine Intake

Caffeine mimics the physical symptoms of anxiety (racing heart, jitteriness) and can accidentally trick your brain into feeling anxious.

Try this today

Swap your second cup of coffee for a decaf or herbal tea today.

Tip 5

Drop the Resistance

Fighting anxiety often makes it stronger. Accepting its presence paradoxically reduces its power over you.

Try this today

Instead of fighting it, imagine the anxiety as a wave washing over you and eventually passing.

Today's Challenge

Take a 10-minute walk outside without listening to music or a podcast. Just observe your surroundings.

"You don't have to control your thoughts. You just have to stop letting them control you."

When to Seek Professional Support

Self-help is a wonderful starting point, but it does not replace professional therapy. If your anxiety symptoms persist and interfere with your daily life, work, or relationships, please reach out to a licensed professional.

Frequently Asked Questions

Will my anxiety ever completely go away?

Anxiety is a normal human emotion, so it will never disappear entirely. The goal is not to eliminate it, but to change your relationship with it so it no longer holds you back.

Why is my anxiety worse in the morning?

Cortisol (the stress hormone) naturally peaks in the morning to help you wake up. For those with anxiety, this normal peak can feel like panic.

Is it anxiety or a heart problem?

Panic and anxiety can mimic heart issues. However, if you are experiencing severe or new chest pain, always consult a medical professional to be safe.

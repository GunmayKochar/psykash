Frequently Asked Questions

What if reading this does not stop the panic?

The goal of the card is not to instantly stop the panic; it is to stop you from adding secondary fear to it. The panic will burn out on its own.

Should I keep a physical copy of this?

Yes. Taking a screenshot of this page or writing these steps down on a card in your wallet ensures you have it even without the internet.

Is it safe to drive during a panic attack?

No. If you feel a panic attack starting while driving, pull over safely, read this card, and wait 15 minutes for the adrenaline to subside.

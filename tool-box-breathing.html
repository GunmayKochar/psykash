Box Breathing

A powerful breathing technique used by Navy SEALs to instantly regulate the nervous system under extreme stress.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is 4 seconds a strict rule?

No. If 4 seconds feels too difficult or makes you dizzy, you can reduce the box to 3 seconds. The important part is the equal rhythm.

Why do I feel lightheaded?

You might be breathing too deeply or too forcefully. Try to make the breaths softer and more relaxed.

How long should I do it for?

Even one minute (about 4 cycles) will have a physiological effect, but 3 to 5 minutes is optimal for deep calming.

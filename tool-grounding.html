5-4-3-2-1 Grounding

A sensory technique to pull your mind away from anxious thoughts and anchor you in the present moment.

Frequently Asked Questions

What if I cannot find something to smell or taste?

Do not panic. Just try to remember your favourite smell, or simply skip to focusing intensely on one of the other senses.

Should I say the items out loud?

Saying them out loud is highly recommended because it engages another area of the brain, making the grounding effect even stronger.

How many times should I repeat this?

Repeat the cycle as many times as necessary. If one round does not lower your heart rate, start again from five.
